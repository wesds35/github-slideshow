## 2. The math to $1,000,000

Starting from $14,000, invested monthly for 120 months:

| Avg. annual return | Required monthly investment |
|---|---|
| 7% | ~$5,615/mo |
| 8% | ~$5,300/mo |
| 10% | ~$4,700/mo |

**The honest read:** on your income alone, sustained ~$5,300/mo is a ~64% savings rate — not realistic with your debt load. There are three ways to close the gap, and you should use all three:

1. **Make it a household number.** You + spouse together clear ~$13,500/mo take-home. $5,300/mo combined is a 39% household savings rate — hard but very doable, especially splitting rent.
2. **Grow income and invest 100% of every raise.** 3% annual raises + growing the side hustle from $225/wk to $500/wk adds ~$1,400/mo by year 5.
3. **Free cash flow by killing debt fast** (Phase 1 below frees ~$1,000+/mo within a year, +$585/mo when the car is paid off).

## 5. Phase 2 — Build the machine (months 10–36)

Every month, in this order (the "money waterfall"):

1. **401(k) to the full employer match** — free money first.
2. **Roth IRA: $583/mo ($7,000/yr max)** — this is where aggressive growth belongs, tax-free forever.
3. **401(k) up to ~15% of gross** if the fund options are decent.
4. **Taxable brokerage: everything left** — automatic transfer the day after payday.

**What to buy (boring on purpose):**
- 70–80% total US market / S&P 500 index (VTI or VOO)
- 10–20% international (VXUS) and/or small-cap value (AVUV)
- 0–5% "casino money" — your SPX calls, single stocks like CRDO. Hard cap. When it's gone, it's gone until next year.
- RTX / CEG / CRDO: fine to keep as part of the 5–20% satellite, but new money goes to index funds.

**When the car is paid off:** the $585/mo goes straight to the brokerage. Drive the car 5+ more years. A new car payment in year 3 is a ~$150k hole in this plan by year 10.

## 6. Phase 3 — Compound and escalate (years 3–10)

- **Invest 100% of every raise** before lifestyle absorbs it (contributions escalate ~4–5%/yr).
- **Scale the side income** toward $500/wk — at your margins that's ~$1,200/mo more investable.
- **Rebalance once a year**, on your birthday. Otherwise don't touch it. No panic selling in crashes — every dollar in at the bottom is the best dollar you'll ever invest.
- **Never carry a credit card balance again.**
